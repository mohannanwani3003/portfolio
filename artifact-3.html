Artifact 03 · AI Training Interaction

Understanding Large Language Models: Training, Capabilities, and Limitations

An interactive learning activity exploring how LLMs learn from patterns, why they can generate plausible but incorrect answers, and when external tools or verification are needed.

Introduction

This artifact documents an interactive AI training exercise completed for AIML-500. The activity used a guided conversation with an AI chatbot to examine how large language models are trained and what they can and cannot do reliably.

Description

The exercise moved through four learning stages: predicting familiar language patterns, exploring the mechanism behind language-model responses, estimating broad tendencies in training data, and classifying tasks as Good Fit or Danger Zone without additional tools.

Stage 1 · Be the ModelCompleted familiar sentence patterns and evaluated the fictional Velmora question.
Stage 2 · Reveal the MechanismDiscussed next-token prediction, pretraining, post-training, and hallucinations.
Stage 3 · Guess the Training MixClassified public, private, academic, multilingual, and time-sensitive information.
Stage 4 · Good Fit / Danger ZoneEvaluated where AI can help and where tools or human verification are important.

Objective

The objective was to connect concepts about LLM training with practical examples, engage critically with AI-generated responses, and recognize when a model's output needs a source, tool, expert, or independent verification.

Process

I participated in a guided, question-by-question interaction with an AI chatbot. I made predictions, classified examples, explained my reasoning, and considered the limitations of relying on model-generated information. One key example was the fictional country of Velmora, where I recognized that there was not enough information to provide a reliable answer.

Tools and Technologies Used

AI tool: ChatGPT, used as an interactive learning partner.

Concepts explored: Large language models, next-token prediction, pretraining, post-training, training-data tendencies, hallucinations, external tools, and AI task suitability.

Deliverable: Completed AI Training Interaction assignment, including the personalized completion summary and personal reflection.

Key Learning Outcomes

  • LLMs generate plausible continuations from learned patterns; their responses are not automatically verified facts.
  • Pretraining and post-training serve different purposes in model development.
  • Training data varies across models and does not imply access to private messages or confidential company information.
  • Live, exact, rare, private, or high-stakes tasks often require external tools, reliable sources, or human review.
  • AI can be useful for drafting, explaining familiar concepts, summarizing supplied material, and brainstorming, while still requiring appropriate checking.

Value Proposition

Unique Value

This artifact demonstrates my ability to engage with AI concepts through practical examples, evaluate AI-generated information critically, and explain the limitations of language models in clear terms.

Relevance

Understanding both the capabilities and limitations of LLMs is important when applying AI in professional environments. The activity reinforced that selecting the right tool and verifying important outputs are part of responsible AI use.

Original project deliverable

The submitted assignment contains the personalized Stage 5 completion summary and personal reflection from the interactive exercise.
