Artifact 01 · AI & ML Timelines

AI Through Time: Understanding the Evolution of Artificial Intelligence

A visual timeline of breakthroughs, setbacks, and the rise of modern AI—from early ideas to deep learning and generative AI.

Introduction

This artifact presents an AI history timeline created for coursework. It organizes key milestones in artificial intelligence and machine learning to show how the field developed over time.

Description

The presentation moves from early questions about machine intelligence and the establishment of AI as a research field through AI winters, expert systems, deep learning, the Transformer architecture, and the wider adoption of generative AI. It also highlights contributors, factors behind AI's progress, applications, and references.

1950s–1960sFoundations and early learning systems
1970s–1990sAI winters and specialized successes
2006–2016Deep learning milestones
2017–todayTransformers and generative AI

Objective

The objective was to communicate important developments in AI in chronological order, explain why selected milestones matter, and provide credible sources for further learning.

Process

The presentation is organized by historical period. Each period introduces significant events with brief explanations, followed by sections on key contributors, the drivers of modern AI, takeaways, and references.
